Cookie Policy

Last updated: 20 July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how CreatorRadar uses cookies and similar technologies. It complements our Privacy Policy and is not legal advice.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites remember preferences, keep you signed in, measure traffic, or support advertising.

Cookies we set

Essential session cookie

When you log in to an invite-only CreatorRadar account, we set a session cookie named cr_session. It authenticates your browser to our servers so you can use the app and admin tools without re-entering credentials on every request. This cookie is required for the Service to function for signed-in users. It is not used for advertising.

Early access form

Submitting the early-access form (name, email, reason) does not itself require a marketing cookie. Form data is sent to our servers and stored for invite evaluation; if email is configured, a notification may be sent to jamiemathieson5@gmail.com.

Third-party cookies — Google AdSense

The public landing page may load Google AdSense using publisher ID ca-pub-2012205573615902. Google and its partners may set or read cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Serve and measure advertisements
  • Limit how often you see an ad
  • Personalise ads where permitted by law and your settings

These cookies are controlled by Google. Learn more at Google Advertising and Google Privacy Policy. You can also visit Google Ads Settings to manage ad personalisation.

How to control cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies via settings. Blocking the cr_session cookie will prevent you from staying logged in. Blocking third-party / advertising cookies may reduce or remove personalised ads but the landing page may still load AdSense scripts where present.

Hosting
