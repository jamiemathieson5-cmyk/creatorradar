Agency lead funnel & scouting DMs

From first watch to first reply — without sounding like spam

A TikTok Live agency lead funnel is simple on paper: source → qualify → message → track → convert. Most teams fail in the middle — scouting DMs that sound automated, or pipelines that never get updated. This guide focuses on the message and funnel habits that keep outreach human.

Map the funnel stages

  • New — found, not contacted
  • Messaged — first DM sent
  • Followed up — one polite bump
  • Replied — conversation started
  • Call / Discord — moving off-platform
  • Closed / not a fit — so you stop looping

Anatomy of a scouting DM that can get a reply

  1. Specific opener — one concrete detail from their Live or niche (not “I love your content”).
  2. Who you are — network / agency name in plain language; no fake urgency.
  3. Why them — fit for your roster or support style in one sentence.
  4. Soft CTA — reply if curious, share a Discord, or book a short chat.
  5. Exit ramp — make it easy to decline so you do not look pushy.

Example shape (customise every time)

“Hey [name] — caught your [game / chat / music] Live earlier; the [specific moment] stood out. I’m [you] with [network]. We work with UK Live creators on [support angle]. If you’re open to a quick chat about whether we’d be a fit, happy to share more — no pressure either way.”

Treat that as a skeleton, not a script. Swap in real details or do not send.

What to avoid

  • Diamond guarantees or “we’ll make you famous” claims
  • Mass templates with wrong handles or niches
  • Stacking five follow-ups in two days
  • Messaging during an obvious meltdown or sensitive moment on stream
