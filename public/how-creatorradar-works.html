How CreatorRadar works

Invite-only lead board for Live creator outreach

CreatorRadar is built for creator networks and agencies that need a quieter way to work Live leads — especially UK-focused prospecting. It is not an open signup product and not a public creator marketplace. Access is invite-only so the board stays useful for teams who are actively scouting.

The core idea: a shared lead board

Instead of dumping handles into personal Notes apps or chaotic group chats, invited users work from an assigned lead view. That keeps ownership clear: who is working a prospect, what status they are in, and what note or draft matters next. The goal is outreach operations — find, contact, track — not another vanity analytics dashboard.

Who it is for

  • Creator networks building or refreshing a Live creator roster
  • Agency / team seats that need shared prospecting ownership
  • Scouts who already DM creators and want the pipeline organised

If you are looking for a free public directory of every LIVE creator, CreatorRadar is not that. See Live lead generation for agencies for how the wider funnel works.

Early access → invite → seat

  1. Request early access on the homepage with your name, email, and why you want in.
  2. We review fit — Creator Network or Agency/Team context matters while capacity is limited.
  3. Invite & account — invited users get login credentials for the app (username + password).
  4. Work leads — use the board for assigned prospects, status, notes, and scouting DM drafting where available.

What you can expect inside

  • Lead-oriented views aimed at outreach, not open browsing for the public
  • Tracking so follow-ups and ownership do not get lost across a team
  • Helpers for drafting scouting messages (you still send outreach yourself)

CreatorRadar does not replace the platforms you already use, your contracts, or your onboarding process. It helps the scouting layer that happens before a creator joins.

Privacy and invite-only design

Because accounts are invite-only, product data is limited to people we have approved. Early access submissions and account sessions are handled as described in our Privacy Policy. The public site may show Google ads; the logged-in product is for invited users.
